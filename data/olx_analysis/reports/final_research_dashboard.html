Core Scientific Principle

ANOMALY ≠ FRAUD | EVIDENCE COUNT ≠ RISK SCORE
TrustLens strictly separates objective forensic measurements from subjective intent. An anomalous listing indicates statistical novelty relative to the observed sample. It does not establish fraud, criminal intent, seller malice, or stolen imagery without independent external ground truth.

TrustLens evaluated listings across independent visual, textual, pricing, OCR, network, and statistical novelty dimensions, proving that multi-modal cross-corroboration provides a superior foundation for marketplace integrity over single-signal heuristic rules.
