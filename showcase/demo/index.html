Screen 9 of 10 — In-Depth Investigation
Investigator Case Study: The Cross-City Service Cluster
A structured review board demonstrating how an investigator analyzes independent signals without automated accusations.

What We Saw

Two listings advertising identical mobile software repair services across Nashik (₹999) and Mumbai (₹1,499), published within the same capture envelope.

What The System Found

  • Exact SHA-256 binary match on promotional flyer image.
  • High lexical similarity (Jaccard similarity > 0.85).
  • Identical OCR banner phrase extracted by Tesseract.
  • DINOv2 embedding cosine similarity > 0.95.

What Remains Unknown

  • Whether a legitimate repair business operates authorized branches in both cities.
  • Whether the listings belong to a single merchant or an unauthorized copycat.
  • Whether physical customer service transactions occurred.

Why It Deserves Human Review

Multi-modal convergence confirms that this is not an isolated random post. It isolates a commercial syndication template spanning metropolitan regions.

Screen 10 of 10 — Scientific Honesty
Research Boundaries & Methodological Limits
TrustLens explicitly documents what the dataset and models cannot establish. Limitations are treated as core engineering constraints.
System BoundaryObserved RealityDefinitive Scientific Boundary
Seller Identity Unobserved in search-card capture Cannot determine common ownership, account coordination, or persistent seller reputation.
Descriptions Not available in search cards All linguistic intelligence is derived exclusively from listing titles.
Ground Truth Labels No fraud outcome database No supervised fraud classifier can be trained. Models isolate statistical novelty, not guilt.
AI Image Provenance No local ground truth Dual detectors yield candidate hypotheses. Disagreements highlight reliance limits.
Marketplace Scope Targeted electronics queries Findings reflect a targeted electronics sample and cannot be generalized to all OLX transactions.
